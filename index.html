Good to know

Before you visit.

A few practical answers to help you plan.

Call 050 078 7348 ↗
What if I don’t know which service to choose?+

Choose “Help me choose” when requesting an appointment, or call us. You can explain what you need without having to choose a specialist yourself.

Is my appointment confirmed when I fill in the form?+

Not yet. Review your request, then send it through WhatsApp or your email app. Our team will contact you to agree a date and time. Please wait for confirmation before travelling.

What should I bring to my first visit?+

If you have them, bring previous test results, scans, referral letters and a list of medicines you take. Write down any questions you would like to ask. The team can tell you whether anything else is needed.

How do I check fees and insurance?+

Please ask our team about consultation fees, test costs, payment options and whether your insurance is accepted before your visit. Costs depend on the care you need.

Where is the clinic, and when can I visit?+

We’re in Adenta, Accra. Please call for current opening hours and an exact location pin before travelling. You can find our phone numbers on the contact page.
