A warm welcome starts here

Your first visit,
one step at a time.

You don’t need to have everything worked out. Here’s a little guidance to help you feel prepared.

Before you come in

Start by requesting an appointment or calling our team. We’ll agree your appointment details with you directly. If you’re unsure which service to choose, tell us — we can help you find a starting point.

  • Confirm your appointment date, time and clinic location.
  • Ask about consultation fees and payment or insurance arrangements.
  • Tell us about any access, language or support needs so we can discuss arrangements.

Bring what you have

Previous results, scans and referral letters can be helpful, along with a list of your medicines. If you don’t have these, ask our team what’s needed for your visit.

Make room for your questions

Your consultation is a chance to talk about what’s worrying you and what you hope for. You can ask your clinician to explain a test, a treatment option, its cost or your follow-up plan.

Before you leave

Check that you understand the agreed next steps, how you’ll get any results, and who to contact with questions.

Good to know

Your questions,
answered.

What if I don’t know which service to choose?+

Choose “Help me choose” when requesting an appointment, or call us. You can explain what you need without having to choose a specialist yourself.

Is my appointment confirmed when I fill in the form?+

Not yet. Review your request, then send it through WhatsApp or your email app. Our team will contact you to agree a date and time. Please wait for confirmation before travelling.

What should I bring to my first visit?+

If you have them, bring previous test results, scans, referral letters and a list of medicines you take. Write down any questions you would like to ask. The team can tell you whether anything else is needed.

How do I check fees and insurance?+

Please ask our team about consultation fees, test costs, payment options and whether your insurance is accepted before your visit. Costs depend on the care you need.

Where is the clinic, and when can I visit?+

We’re in Adenta, Accra. Please call for current opening hours and an exact location pin before travelling. You can find our phone numbers on the contact page.
