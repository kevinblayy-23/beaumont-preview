About this service

Uterine fibroids are among the most common gynaecological conditions and can significantly affect quality of life, fertility and reproductive health. Our Fibroid Centre provides specialised diagnosis, treatment and long-term management for women living with fibroids.

We use advanced imaging to assess the size, location and impact of your fibroids. Based on your symptoms, reproductive goals and overall health, we develop a treatment plan that is right for you.

We focus on effective treatment while preserving your ability to have children whenever possible, and on relieving symptoms such as heavy bleeding, pelvic pain and fertility difficulties.

Treatment options

  • Detailed fibroid assessment and imaging
  • Medical management
  • Fertility-preserving therapies
  • Laparoscopic (keyhole) myomectomy
  • Open myomectomy for complex cases
  • Long-term follow-up

Symptoms worth checking

  • Heavy or prolonged periods
  • Pelvic pain or a feeling of pressure
  • Needing to pass urine often, or bloating
  • A swelling in the lower abdomen
  • Difficulty conceiving or repeated miscarriage
