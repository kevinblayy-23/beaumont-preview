About this service

We are committed to providing modern surgical care through minimally invasive techniques that reduce discomfort and speed up recovery.

Our specialists perform a wide range of laparoscopic (keyhole) and hysteroscopic procedures. These typically involve smaller incisions, less pain after surgery, shorter hospital stays and a quicker return to your normal activities.

We combine surgical expertise with modern technology to deliver safe, effective, patient-focused care for many gynaecological conditions.

Procedures we perform

  • Diagnostic and operative laparoscopy
  • Hysteroscopy
  • Myomectomy
  • Ovarian cyst surgery
  • Tubal surgery
  • Adhesiolysis (removal of scar tissue)

Why minimally invasive?

  • Smaller incisions and scars
  • Less pain after surgery
  • Shorter hospital stay
  • Faster return to work and family life
