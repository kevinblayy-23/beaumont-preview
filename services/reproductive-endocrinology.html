About this service

Hormonal health plays a critical role in fertility, reproductive function and overall wellbeing. Our Reproductive Endocrinology & Hormonal Health Centre diagnoses and treats complex hormonal disorders that affect women at different stages of life.

Through thorough evaluation and personalised treatment, our specialists help you restore hormonal balance, improve reproductive health, manage symptoms and enjoy a better quality of life.

Our goal is to give every patient the knowledge and care they need for optimal, long-term health.

Conditions we treat

  • Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)
  • Irregular periods
  • Absent periods (amenorrhoea)
  • Premature ovarian insufficiency
  • Diminished ovarian reserve
  • Raised prolactin (hyperprolactinaemia)
  • Thyroid disorders affecting fertility
  • Menopause care
  • Adolescent gynaecology

When to book

  • Your periods are irregular, very light or have stopped
  • You have acne, excess hair growth or weight changes alongside irregular cycles
  • You have hot flushes, night sweats or other menopause symptoms
  • You have nipple discharge and aren't breastfeeding
  • You're struggling to conceive and suspect a hormonal cause
