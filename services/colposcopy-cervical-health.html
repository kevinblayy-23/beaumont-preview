About this service

Cervical health plays a vital role in women's overall wellbeing. Our Colposcopy & Cervical Health Clinic focuses on the prevention, early detection, diagnosis and treatment of cervical abnormalities.

Regular screening and timely treatment reduce the risk of cervical cancer. Our experienced team provides compassionate care in a comfortable, confidential environment, so you can take proactive steps for your health.

What we offer

  • Pap smear screening
  • HPV testing
  • Colposcopy
  • Cervical biopsy
  • LLETZ / LEEP treatment
  • Management of cervical dysplasia

When to book

  • You're due for routine cervical screening
  • You've had an abnormal smear or a positive HPV result
  • You bleed after sex or between periods
  • You have unusual vaginal discharge
  • You need follow-up after previous treatment
