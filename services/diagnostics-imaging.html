About this service

Accurate diagnosis is the foundation of effective care. Our Diagnostic & Imaging department provides imaging and laboratory support so that clinical decisions are well informed.

Using modern equipment and experienced professionals, we deliver timely, reliable results — whether we're monitoring a pregnancy, assessing fertility or investigating a medical condition.
